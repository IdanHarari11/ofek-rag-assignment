# Agent FAQ – Ofek Insurance (internal, for licensed agents)

**Q: Can an agent approve coverage for a pre-existing medical condition in travel insurance?**
No. Agents must forward the customer's health declaration (Form 3002) to Medical Underwriting. Medical Underwriting responds within 3 business days. Agents must not tell the customer that coverage is approved before written confirmation.

**Q: What happens to my commission if the customer cancels?**
If the policy is cancelled within 120 days of issue, the full commission is clawed back. Between 121 days and 12 months, the clawback is pro-rata.

**Q: Can I update the customer's address on their behalf?**
Yes, with a signed power of attorney (Form 4420) on file.

**Q: Can I offer a discount above the standard tariff?**
Agents may grant up to 5% at their discretion. Anything above that requires sales manager approval.

**Q: Where do I find the current policy wording?**
On the agents' portal, under "Policy Documents". Always check the version date.
